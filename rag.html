What is happening?

🧠 Important Concept

  • Embedding = numerical representation of meaning.
  • Vector DB = stores embeddings for fast similarity search.
  • Retriever = finds potentially relevant chunks.
  • Reranker = improves ordering of retrieved chunks.
  • LLM = generates the final response using retrieved context.
